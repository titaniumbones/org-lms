This announcement exists to exercise every layout and styling feature that
~ox-canvashtml~ currently supports. The image below is a *local file* on disk;
posting uploads it to the course Files area and rewrites the link.

#+CAPTION: Lake Ontario, March. A float-right figure with a caption.
#+ATTR_HTML: :class float-right
[[file:20100310_95.JPG]]

Text set beside a floated figure wraps around it. This paragraph is here to
give the float something to wrap against, so you can see that the ~float-right~
class survived Canvas's HTML sanitizer as an inlined ~style~ attribute rather
than a class reference to a stylesheet Canvas never loads. The figure is capped
at 35% of the container width.

** Alert boxes

#+BEGIN_canvas-alert-info
*Info.* Meet at the Old Vic steps at 08:45. The bus leaves at 09:00 sharp.
#+END_canvas-alert-info

#+BEGIN_canvas-alert-warning
*Warning.* Bring waterproof boots. The shoreline path floods at high water.
#+END_canvas-alert-warning

#+BEGIN_canvas-alert-success
*Success.* Everyone who submitted a permission form by Friday is confirmed.
#+END_canvas-alert-success

#+BEGIN_canvas-alert-danger
*Danger.* Do not walk out onto the ice, under any circumstances.
#+END_canvas-alert-danger

A single paragraph can carry an alert class too, without a block:

#+ATTR_HTML: :class canvas-alert-info
This paragraph got its styling from ~#+ATTR_HTML: :class canvas-alert-info~.
